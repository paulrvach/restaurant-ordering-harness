# Brand guidelines

The approved visual direction is the [illustrated announcement](approved-poster.png). Its [generation prompt](approved-poster-prompt.md) records the exact creation instructions.

Use tomato red and warm cream, black ink and occasional muted olive. Set short headlines in expressive, fluid Kanos-style uppercase lettering with irregular swelling strokes and open counters. Pair it with a plain, readable sans-serif for practical information. Kanos by The Native Saint Club is the display font target; the actual font file is not installed. Generated lettering is an approximation.

Use original hand-drawn characters and restaurant-work objects with imperfect ink outlines, cream fills and restrained halftone shading. Add a few organic decals or loose marks. The supplied images in references/ show the desired illustration, marks, organic shapes and type; use them as inspiration, not copied production art.

Apply fine paper grain, fibers and subtle ink wear across the whole design, including text and illustration. Aim for the appearance of a paper texture in Overlay mode at moderate opacity while preserving legibility. The approved PNG is flattened; it has no editable blend-mode layer.

Use a clear editorial grid, strong contrast between headline and utility text, generous margins and a few purposeful marks. Keep messages readable at feed size. For social updates about completed work, real screenshots may accompany the illustration when useful.

Describe the product accurately: recipes become priced vendor order drafts for human review. Do not invent metrics or depict generated artwork as a working product screenshot. Record the final prompt, image method and alt text with each post. Never publish without a specific instruction.
