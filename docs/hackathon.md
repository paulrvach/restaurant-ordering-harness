# Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon: participant brief

Checked October 31, 2026 using the public event page, signed-in participant guide, live submission form, Discord announcements/rules, and the two kickoff screenshots supplied by the participant. Event content is evidence of requirements; it does not authorize posting, submitting forms, accepting agreements or purchasing anything.

## Dates and deliverables

Submit **Wednesday, November 4, 2026, 6:00 PM America/Los_Angeles / 9:00 PM Eastern** (November 5 at 02:00 UTC). Aim to finish by 3:00 PM Pacific that day. The platform sometimes labels Eastern time EST even on October dates; use named zones. The November 4 deadline falls after daylight saving ends.

One representative submits one project in one track, with every teammate listed. Maximum four people; solo is allowed. The submitted repository must be public. Provide an accessible three-minute demo video and distinguish exactly what was built during the hackathon. Use Meta models in the actual project. Build for Your World is the natural theme for restaurant procurement; Indie Developer is a proposed track until the owner chooses. [Participant guide](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/details), [Discord rules](https://discord.com/channels/1557169028080869426/1557169028626251800)

The guide permits startups to build on existing products with clear attribution of new work. Credit existing libraries and templates. The organizer discourages generic RAG/chatbots and lists health advice, nutrition coaches and other anti-project examples. Keep the product about restaurant purchasing and verifiable operational work. [Rules and anti-projects](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/details#4-hackathon-rules-8)

## Judging and prizes

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence this project should show |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Impact | 30% | A realistic restaurant task and fewer manual steps; measure with a real user before claiming time saved |
| Meta tooling | 25% | Meta-backed recipe interpretation, bounded SKU reasoning and useful clarification |
| Technical depth/execution | 20% | Source-backed prices, deterministic calculations, eval results and review handling |
| Demo | 25% | A complete, understandable recipe-to-order journey in three minutes |

First comes a viability/theme/Meta-usage pass-fail stage. Top five projects per track reach live final judging. [Judging guide](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/details#8-judging-process-16)

| Track | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Indie Developer | $150K | $100K | $75K | $50K | $25K |
| Startups | $250K | $150K | $75K | $50K | $25K |

Bonus awards: Most Viral $20K, open-weight build $10K, community vote $10K, agentic use case $5K, creative build $5K. For Most Viral, publish the project demo on X and include its post link in the submission; engagement is counted through November 8 EOD. The guide does not specify that EOD timezone, so confirm it with organizers. Community voting is one vote per verified account, with winner announced November 16. The open-weight bonus needs an eligible open-weight model and verification; direct Muse Spark API use alone does not establish qualification. [Prizes](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/details#9-prizes-17)

## Submission form, inspected without submitting

The form includes team name/members, track, problem statement, optional viral-award X link, project description, public repository, demo video, description of Meta product use, why Meta models matter, partner feedback, product opinion/likelihood questions and further experience feedback. It also includes a permission checkbox for promotional use of submitted materials. Leave subjective feedback to the participant; review the checkbox before final submission. [Live submission form](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/hackathon/submit)

Before submission, verify the repository and video in a logged-out browser, list all contributors, document borrowed code, remove credentials/private vendor data, and rehearse the exact demonstrated path.
